\section{Methods}
\label{sec:methods}

Let $y_t$ be the reference mass concentration and $x_{i,t}$ the raw count
of node $i$ at hour $t$. We fit the \(\kappa\)-corrected model

\begin{equation}
  \label{eq:model}
  y_t = \alpha_i \cdot \frac{x_{i,t}}{1 + \kappa \, a_w(t)} + \beta_i
\end{equation}

by least squares, where $a_w$ is the water activity derived from relative
humidity. Residuals are reported per node; see also \cite{difantonio2018}
for the hygroscopic growth correction.
